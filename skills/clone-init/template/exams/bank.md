# Exam bank

`[ ]` not solved · `[x score/25]` solved · `[re]` under 22, solve again. /exam picks from here and appends a harder follow-up every time.

## Starter questions
- [ ] A bug report lands in the team channel. Take it from first read to a closed ticket — where you look, what you tell which harness, what you post back
- [ ] A new ticket is assigned to you. Walk it from "in progress" to merged — branch, review, ticket transition
- [ ] Which repos does the owner work in, and what do they do in each

## Scenarios (role and constraint lenses)
